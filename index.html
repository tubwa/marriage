FAQ

Common questions

Who can create a profile?

Banjara Christian men and women aged 18 or above. A parent or guardian may also fill the form using the contact person fields.

What should I keep ready?

Your personal, education and family details, a recent photograph, and a mobile and WhatsApp number.

Can I leave some fields blank?

Yes. Fields marked optional, such as blood group, annual income and the cultural and partner expectation sections, can be skipped.

What happens after I submit?

You see a reference ID on screen. Please keep it. Your details are recorded with the association, which contacts you using the numbers you provided.

How do I contact the association?

Write to us at the email shown in the footer, or follow us on Instagram and YouTube.
